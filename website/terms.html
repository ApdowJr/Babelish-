Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-10-23 · applies to Babelish 3.4 and later

This is the public copy of the mobile app terms. This static website provides download and legal information only; Babelish lessons run in the Android and iPhone app, not in a browser.

The short version

Babelish is a free language-learning app. Use it, learn, and don’t abuse it. Your practice data stays on your device; only progress and vocabulary sync to your own account. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Your account

You may use Babelish without an account in demo mode, or create one to sync progress across devices. Keep your credentials private — you are responsible for activity under your account. You must be old enough to consent to data processing where you live (13+ in most places, 16+ in some).

Acceptable use

  • Don’t attempt to overload, scrape or reverse the AI endpoints.
  • Don’t use the tutor to generate illegal content, or to harass another person.
  • Don’t try to bypass the daily limits; they exist so a free service stays free for everyone.
  • Don’t upload content you have no right to share.

The AI tutor is a learning aid

Tutor replies come from open-weight language models, run on our server or on your device. They can be wrong, incomplete or oddly phrased. Nothing the tutor says is professional advice — not medical, legal, financial or safety advice. Babelish never fakes a pronunciation score: when there is no transcript to compare against, the app says so.

Free service, honest limits

Babelish is free and has no ads. Tutor messages and voice transcriptions have daily limits, shown in the app when you reach them; lessons, review and the phrasebook keep working without them. We may change limits or features, and we try to say so in the changelog rather than silently.

Your content, our software

What you write and record stays yours. You give us only the permission needed to transcribe your speech and answer your messages while you use the app. The Babelish name, mark and source code belong to their authors and are provided under the licence in the repository (see LICENSE).

Availability and liability

The app is provided “as is”. We do not promise that the tutor server will always be reachable, or that a model will always answer. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss arising from use of the app. Nothing here limits rights you have under mandatory consumer law.

Ending it

You can stop using Babelish at any time, and delete your account in Settings → Danger zone. We may suspend an account that abuses the service, and we will tell you why.

Privacy

What is stored, what syncs and what never leaves the device is described in the Privacy Policy. It is part of these terms.
