Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23 · this page is the same text as Settings → Privacy policy in the app

This page is written in English, like the in-app policy. The app itself is not a website: it runs on your phone, and this page exists so the policy is reachable without installing anything.

The short version

Your practice stays on your phone. Babelish has no ads, no third-party analytics, and no data brokers. The only thing that ever syncs to a server is your progress and vocabulary — in your Supabase account, protected by Row Level Security (RLS). Microphone audio is transcribed on demand and never stored.

What lives only on your device

  • Practice events, XP, streaks, league standing (computed locally)
  • Your learner profile (name, goals, interests)
  • Reminder settings (scheduling is a local OS notification — no push server)
  • The offline curriculum for every language (bundled in the app)

None of this leaves the device unless you sign in, and even then only the items in the next section sync.

What syncs when you sign in

DataWhereWhy
Lesson progress (scores, timestamps) Supabase PostgreSQL (user_progress) Resume on another device
Vocabulary / spaced-repetition boxes Supabase PostgreSQL (user_vocab) Keep review queues correct
Weekly XP (a number, and a week key) Stored locally for now Your rank on the weekly board

The weekly board is privacy-first by design: you see your own rank and the number of participants — never another learner’s identity, and they never see yours. Reports you file (“this sounds wrong”) are queued with your user id so reviewers can follow up; they are never shown to other learners.

Microphone audio

  • Word-match feedback is computed on your device from the transcript; it is not acoustic pronunciation scoring.
  • Optional cloud speech-to-text (only if an OpenAI-compatible endpoint is configured) receives the audio for transcription only; the audio is discarded immediately and only the transcribed phrase is kept as practice content.
  • With no STT configured, audio never leaves the device — and the app says so instead of pretending otherwise.

AI tutor content

Tutor replies use the Supabase AI Edge Function when a provider is configured with server-side secrets, or the clearly labelled bundled offline coach when no live model is available. Conversation text is sent to the configured provider to generate the reply and is not stored by Babelish. Every AI-generated item that could reach the curriculum passes the human approval gate before publication — AI never publishes itself.

Children

Babelish is general-audience (12+). It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. No ads, no tracking, no behavioral profiling — for anyone, of any age.

Your rights

  • Export everything the app knows about you: Settings → Data & storage → Export my data (JSON via the system share sheet).
  • Delete your account: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. After confirmation, a protected Supabase Edge Function deletes your signed-in account; the app then clears local learning data and saved conversations. If the server cannot confirm deletion, the app reports failure.
  • No cookie banners, because there is nothing to consent to.

Operators & subprocessors
